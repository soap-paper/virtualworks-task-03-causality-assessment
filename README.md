# VirtualWorks Lab — Task 3: Causality Assessment of Drug Reactions

## 📌 Overview

This repository contains my submission for **Task 3 of the VirtualWorks Lab Pharmacovigilance Internship**.

The task focuses on **causality assessment** — evaluating how strongly an adverse event can be linked to a suspected medicine.

Since no patient dataset was provided with the task, I created **three simulated case scenarios** for training purposes.

---

## 🧪 Cases Covered

| Case | Suspected Drug | Reaction | WHO-UMC Assessment | Naranjo Score |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| CA-001 | Ibuprofen | Generalized urticaria | Certain | 9 |
| CA-002 | Lisinopril | Persistent dry cough | Probable / Likely | 6 |
| CA-003 | Metformin | Diarrhea and abdominal discomfort | Possible | 2 |

---

## 🔍 Assessment Factors Considered

- Temporal relationship
- Known association with the medicine
- Dechallenge
- Rechallenge
- Alternative causes
- Relevant patient history
- Clinical outcome
- Naranjo algorithm
- WHO-UMC causality classification

## 🎯 Learning Objective

This task helped me understand that causality assessment is based on the **strength of evidence**, not only on whether an event occurred after a medicine was taken.

The cases demonstrate how stronger evidence such as a positive dechallenge and rechallenge can support a higher level of causality, while competing explanations or incomplete information can reduce the strength of the assessment.
